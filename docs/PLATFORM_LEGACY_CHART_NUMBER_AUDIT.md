# Legacy anatomy and chart-number source audit — 30 September 2026

This inventories chart, graph and prominent numeric families **outside** `web/src/platform` for requirements #41–42. It is a source audit, not browser acceptance or evidence that every family works on Render. The active `web/index.html` loads `web/src/platform/app.js`; `web/src/studio/app.js` is retained code and is **not** the current entry point. The platform embeds `web/anatomy.html`. Its normal opening is the atlas alone; `web/src/session/boot.js` mounts the older recorder, posture check and movement screening only for an explicit `?legacy` or `?session=` URL. A session bundle may be real or explicitly marked synthetic. The platform chart audit is in [PLATFORM_CHART_NUMBER_AUDIT.md](PLATFORM_CHART_NUMBER_AUDIT.md).

“Default” below means visible once the named surface is opened, not that the normal studio dashboard opens that surface. “Comparison” means an actual earlier observation of the same subject, rather than two values from a reference model or two phases of an animation.

## Highest-priority acceptance follow-ups

1. The **five vs nine** section description is corrected in code; verify that the model-surface/fsaverage/no-personal-MRI explanation stays adjacent to all nine sections in embedded and full-screen WebGL views.
2. The old posture coaching-band, normalized-outline and movement radar wording is corrected in code; review real and demo archived results in browser for any remaining unsupported interpretation.
3. Keep explicit-session camera scores, subjective coach scores, authored animation roles, and atlas-network numbers visually and semantically separate from the active platform client's measured-progress charts.
4. Test the anatomy atlas in a WebGL-capable browser, including section thumbnail meaning, selection, labels, narrow viewport, and real vs synthetic session context. Then verify any supported archived `?session=` flows with source data; source inspection alone does not satisfy #41–42.
